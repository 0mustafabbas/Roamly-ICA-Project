# Roamly - Smart Trip Planner

## Concept & Purpose
Roamly is a travel-planning web application that allows users to organize their travels and estimate their overall costs all in one location. It combines trip activities, itinerary planning, and budget calculations to make traveling easier and more affordable.

## Target Users & Problem Solved
- **Target Users:** University students and young adults, budget travelers, groups of friends planning trips, and people who want to organize their travel expenses.
- **Problem:** Travelers often struggle to plan a trip because they need to search for destinations, activities, schedules, and costs across different websites, making it difficult to organize daily plans and estimate total trip costs.
- **Solution:** Roamly brings destination selection, activities, itinerary planning, and cost estimation together in a single platform.
