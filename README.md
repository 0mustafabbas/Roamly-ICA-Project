# Roamly - Smart Trip Planner

## Concept & Purpose
Roamly is a travel-planning web application that allows users to organize their travels and estimate their overall costs all in one location[cite: 39]. It combines trip activities, itinerary planning, and budget calculations to make traveling easier and more affordable[cite: 39].

## Target Users & Problem Solved
- **Target Users:** University students and young adults, budget travelers, groups of friends planning trips, and people who want to organize their travel expenses[cite: 39].
- **Problem:** Travelers often struggle to plan a trip because they need to search for destinations, activities, schedules, and costs across different websites, making it difficult to organize daily plans and estimate total trip costs[cite: 39].
- **Solution:** Roamly brings destination selection, activities, itinerary planning, and cost estimation together in a single platform[cite: 39].
